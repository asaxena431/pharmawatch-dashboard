A 62-year-old female with atrial fibrillation was started on warfarin 5 mg daily.
Two weeks later she developed epistaxis and easy bruising. She was also taking
metformin 500 mg twice daily for type 2 diabetes and reported nausea after meals.
Lisinopril 10 mg was added for hypertension, after which she experienced a dry
cough. She denied any chest pain or shortness of breath. Amoxicillin was
prescribed for a urinary tract infection and she subsequently developed a
widespread rash and pruritus.
